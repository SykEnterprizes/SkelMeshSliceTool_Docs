## Cap Generation
Cap generation is the process of creating cap geometry using the edgeloops found during mesh extraction

The user has direct control over all aspects of the geometry in regards to:
- Fan vs Dome shape
- Protrude or invert the geometry
- Procedural Noise
- Inner Ring additions
- Fade Ring additions can protrude or invert geometry
- Constriction or flaring of the Fade Rings
- Separate Materials can be applied to Fade Ring Geometry and Fan/Dome geometry
- UV controls for the applied Materials
- Bridging between caps

Reverse Caps
---
Reverse Caps can be generated as a direct reversal of the created cap
and fit together like a socket

They are designed to work as simplified Gibs

Each Reverse Cap has the same user controls as the Standard cap
